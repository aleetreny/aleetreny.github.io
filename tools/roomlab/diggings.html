THE DIGGINGS — six rooms, three traces

1:1 traces of three of 0_mem0ry's own makeshift rooms, from makeshift.png and makeshift_roomtiles.png. Every object is the sprite the artist used at the position it is in the render: 7.8 %, 5.5 % and 8.9 % of pixels differ inside the outline, which is the closest any room in the habitat has come. Each digging is its reference's own size, so the trace drops in without moving anything. The only thing that varies between two diggings sharing a reference is the lamp — the map gives every private door its own warm.
